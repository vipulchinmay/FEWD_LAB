Our Story

Founded in 2020, EcoShop began with a simple idea: make sustainable living accessible to everyone.

Our founder, Emma Chen, was frustrated by the lack of affordable, high-quality eco-friendly products in the market. Most sustainable alternatives were either too expensive or difficult to find, creating barriers for people who wanted to reduce their environmental footprint.

Starting with just 5 products in her garage, Emma built EcoShop into what it is today - a trusted source for sustainable everyday items that don't compromise on quality or affordability.

Our Mission

We believe that small changes, when made by many, create massive positive impact.

Our mission is to empower consumers to make eco-conscious choices by providing sustainable alternatives to everyday products. We're committed to:

  • Sourcing materials ethically and sustainably
  • Reducing waste throughout our supply chain
  • Educating our community about environmental issues
  • Supporting environmental initiatives worldwide
  • Creating a transparent business model

Our Journey
